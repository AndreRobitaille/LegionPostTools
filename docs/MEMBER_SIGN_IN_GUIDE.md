# How to Sign In to Your Post's Website

LegionPostTools does not use passwords. Each time you sign in, the website can send a
one-time link and code to your email address.

## Before You Begin

You need:

- the web address provided by your Post; and
- the email address listed on your American Legion membership record.

If your National roster status is Active or Grace and your roster has a usable email
address that belongs only to you, the Post website normally creates and enables your
login account automatically during a roster import.

## Sign In with Email

1. Open your Post's LegionPostTools website.
2. Enter the email address on your American Legion membership record.
3. Select **Send my sign-in email**.
4. Keep that page open and check your email.
5. Open the message from LegionPostTools and use either of these choices:
   - Select **Continue to LegionPostTools**, then select **Finish signing in**.
   - Or return to the page you kept open, enter the 8-digit code from the email, and
     select **Finish signing in**.

The link and code work once and expire after 15 minutes. Using one makes the other stop
working. If they expire, return to the sign-in page and request a new email.

## If the Email Does Not Arrive

First:

1. Wait a few minutes.
2. Check your Spam or Junk folder.
3. Make sure you entered the email address on your American Legion membership record.

For privacy, the website shows the same “check your email” message even when an address
does not have an enabled login account. If no email arrives, contact your Commander,
Adjutant, or the person who manages the Post's website.

You can say:

> I am trying to sign in to the Post website, but I did not receive the sign-in email.
> Please check that my login is enabled and that my American Legion membership record
> has my correct email address.

## If Your Email Address Is Wrong

Tell your Commander or Adjutant. They can help correct the email address in your
American Legion membership record at National Headquarters. The Post will then bring the
correction into LegionPostTools with its next roster import and make sure your login uses
the corrected address.

This may not happen immediately. Ask the person helping you to tell you when the
correction has reached the Post website.

## Keep Your Sign-in Email Private

Do not forward the sign-in email or give its code to anyone. If you receive a sign-in
email that you did not request, ignore it.

## The “Add a Passkey” Message After You Sign In

The first time you sign in, you may see a box at the top of the page that says **A
passkey is not a password**.

A passkey really is **not another password**:

- You do not make up a word.
- You do not type it or memorize it.
- Your phone, computer, browser, or password manager stores it safely for you.
- When you sign in, you approve it with the same fingerprint, face, device PIN, or
  computer sign-in that you already use.

Most current phones, computers, and browsers support passkeys. Set one up only on a
personal or trusted device.

To use one, select **Set up a passkey** and follow the message from your device. The exact
screen looks different on an iPhone, Android phone, Windows computer, Mac, or password
manager. If the device asks where to save the passkey, its built-in choice is usually
free and works well.

If you already use a password manager such as 1Password, it may offer to save the passkey
there so you can use it on your other devices. That is optional. You do not need to buy
1Password or another password manager to use LegionPostTools.

If you are unsure or do not want to set one up, select **Not now**. Email sign-in will
continue to work exactly as before. You can add a passkey later from your profile.
