# Buddy Checks on the website

**Menu → Your work → Buddy Checks** brings together check-ins, taking care of
one another, and agreed follow-up. Buddy Checks also support suicide prevention.
Membership renewal and dues are a separate workflow.

Calling tools require the current Buddy Checks pilot permission and an enabled
account. Current coordinators can review Post activity, care coverage, and the
monthly county total without pilot access. Coordination reuses the existing full
private-outreach authority; the pilot grant alone does not expose other callers'
notes. Contact export remains a separate permission.

## Choose someone and prepare a call

Open **Buddy Checks**. Due callbacks appear first. The suggested person is based on
recorded contact or two explicitly covered meeting absences. When there is too little
history, the page offers random available choices and says so. Missing history never
means that someone has never received a call. **Find a member** lets you choose a
permitted person by name.

Open the person to review current contact availability, your recent checks, callbacks,
and available attendance evidence. Recent contact, another caller's assignment, an
agreed callback, or paused contact can make a new call inappropriate. Phone and email
links open your device's application; they do not save a completed check.

## Record what happened

Choose **Record a Buddy Check**, enter the actual contact date/time, channel, result,
and brief notes. Times use the Post's timezone. An optional agreed callback is saved
with the check. If you have an active Buddy Check drive assignment, choose that drive
to preserve the assignment with the result.

Keep sensitive medical or financial details out of these notes. Buddy Checks are
welfare outreach; record dues solicitation in a separate renewal contact.

The check appears on the person's preparation page and under **Your check history**,
including checks saved in the iPhone app and across membership years. **Correct this
check** preserves the original and saves a later revision. Date-only earlier records
keep their original precision. A correction does not change an agreed callback.

## Keep agreed callbacks

Use **Your callbacks** to prepare a call or **Manage callback** to reschedule, mark it
complete, or cancel it. A completed callback does not automatically record a physical
contact; use **Record the actual contact** when a call happened. New callback times
must be in the future. The website and app use the same saved callbacks.

Earlier date-only callback notes remain separately labelled under **Dates recorded on
earlier checks**. They are historical contact facts, not fabricated timed appointments.

## Defer a suggestion

On a person's preparation page, **Defer suggestion for seven days** sets that person
aside from your suggestions. **Deferred suggestions → Restore suggestion** undoes it.
This is personal to your account and does not cancel a callback or another caller's work.

## Review checks recorded by other callers

Open **Menu → Your work → Buddy Checks → Post activity**. A coordinator without
personal pilot access opens directly to Post activity. **Officer tools → Buddy Checks**
is another route into this workspace. Current full-information authority controls
coordination; a calling pilot grant alone does not expose other callers' private notes.

Post activity shows Buddy Checks recorded on the website, in the mobile app, or from
returned paper. Search by member or choose a result. Open the member's name for
**Buddy Check history**, including date, result, caller attribution, and brief notes.
These records span membership years. Renewal contacts and dues do not appear here.
Mobile and website records share the installation's database; the development database
is separate from production.

## Prepare the monthly county report

Open **County report**, choose **Month** and **Year**, then **Show county total**.
County receives one number: **Buddy Checks performed** where the person was reached.
Repeated checks count separately, while retries/corrections of the same check count
once. A reached conversation arranging a callback or requesting no further contact
counts. Unanswered attempts, wrong numbers, not-attempted entries, administrative
reviews, care reviews, and callback scheduling/completion alone do not count.

The period uses the actual contact date in the Post's calendar, rather than the date
someone entered it. The live total reflects corrections and says when it was calculated.
Review the number before reporting it. County reporting contains no member names,
caller notes, extra-care details, or dues statistics; the website does not submit it.

## Review internal care coverage

Open **Care coverage**. The internal summary shows available members, recorded
conversations within the chosen look-back, people needing a check-in, and no-contact
requests. The look-back defaults to 60 days and can be changed. This is independent
of renewal standing and the county's single monthly statistic.

Each member shows the last recorded conversation and last recorded meeting attendance.
Missing history stays **unknown**. An absence alert requires the latest two covered
registers to be complete and explicitly absent; unknown/incomplete/present/excused
entries interrupt that alert. **All members** includes the current coverage population;
removed and deceased roster entries are excluded from new outreach.

Use **No contact requested** to review Buddy Check preferences. From a member's Buddy
Check history, **Record a contact preference** saves the member's request and its
source. Restoring contact requires the member's actual agreement. Routine outreach
is paused while a no-contact request is current.

## Record private extra-care follow-up

Open a member's Buddy Check history and choose **Record care follow-up**. Record a
short practical observation/next step, choose a current coordinator as **Follow-up
owner**, and set **Review by**. These are human concerns, not automated risk scores
or diagnoses. Keep detailed medical information out of the note.

**Extra care follow-up** on the coverage page includes the open reviews and identifies
due dates or an owner whose coordination access ended. Complete or reassign the review
by recording a later update; earlier notes remain attributed and preserved. An outdated
form cannot overwrite a newer review, and a retry cannot create another revision.
Care notes stay private to authorized coordinators, outside personal caller pages,
mobile responses, general profiles, and county reporting.

## Coordinate callers and meeting attendance

**Buddy Check drives** keeps Buddy callers, instructions, and call sheets separate
from membership drives. Buddy drive pages do not use renewal-year or dues controls.
Their returned results appear in Buddy Check history. Personal callers retain only
their assigned work and notes.

Coordinators with pilot access also see **Meeting attendance**. Choose a past meeting
and review the actual member list and source. Search filters the visible rows while
every member's status is saved. Mark coverage complete only when all covered entries
have been checked. Register revisions remain separate from officer roll call and
minutes. Attendance is also available from the relevant **Meeting** page.

## Manage the iPhone app's account access

Open **Your profile → Connected apps → Buddy Checks app connections**. It lists
active short-lived and remembered connections. Disconnecting ends that connection's
access, including its remembered sign-in. Saved checks and callbacks remain, and your
website session stays signed in. Start connection sign-in from the iPhone app.

Current access is checked again on each read and save. If a record changes while a
form is open, reload and review the current version. Invalid forms retain your entered
values so you can correct them. No new account or role grants are created by using this
workspace.
