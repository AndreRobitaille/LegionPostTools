## What the actor did and saw

Record actual navigation, visible wording, mistakes, recovery, and who appeared to
act next. Link local screenshots and any repro video. Avoid fabricated quotations,
emotion, or human completion-time claims.

## Independent saved-state and later-read checks

The actor records later visible reads here. After the actor report is complete, the
supervisor adds persisted-state evidence, including relevant denial, old sessions,
immutable revisions, and member-visible content. Keep supervisor evidence out of the
actor's task context. Name any offline substitute or untested boundary.

## Findings

For each finding, give: factual observation, reproducible steps, expected outcome from
policy or the task, evidence, impact on the role, and a proposed improvement. Label
unreproduced concerns as candidates. Separate tooling failures and missing task facts.

## Questions for real officers

List assumptions requiring a Commander, Adjutant, or administrator's actual experience.
Record proposed UI changes separately from verified application defects.
